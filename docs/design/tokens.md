# Luxury Design Tokens

## Palette Specifications
- **Obsidian Black (`#0D0D0D`)**: The primary darkness of luxury packaging.
- **Charcoal (`#161616`)**: Secondary dark surface for cards, dropdowns, inputs.
- **Graphite (`#222222`)**: Border and subtle hover states.
- **Alabaster Cream (`#FAF8F5`)**: Primary light text, light backgrounds, flacon cards.
- **Champagne Gold (`#C5A880`)**: Signature metallic accent for borders, badges, buttons.
- **Muted Gold (`#8E734F`)**: Secondary understated gold for subtle accents.

## Typography
- **Headlines**: `Cormorant Garamond`, `Cinzel`, `Playfair Display` (serif).
- **Controls & Body**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter` (sans-serif).
- **Codes, SKUs, Tracking**: `JetBrains Mono` (monospace).
