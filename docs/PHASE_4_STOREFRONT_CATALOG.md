# Phase 4: Storefront Core Pages & Olfactory Shopping Experience

## Objective
Build the public luxury storefront for PHILZ SIGNATURE inspired by Dior and Tom Ford, featuring smooth micro-animations, the interactive Olfactory Pyramid, comprehensive filtering, and the slide-out cart drawer.

## Key Deliverables
1. **Homepage**: Parallax hero billboard, Curated Collections grid, Bestsellers showcase, Olfactory Pyramid explorer, Brand Story highlights, Customer testimonials, VIP newsletter invitation.
2. **Shop Page**: Multi-dimensional filter drawer/sidebar (Fragrance Family, Gender, Olfactory Notes, Price range, Collections), sorting, and grid/list view toggles.
3. **Collections Page**: Editorial gallery of fragrance collections with hero storytelling.
4. **Product Details Page**:
   - Multi-image zoom gallery and campaign video reel.
   - Interactive Olfactory Pyramid (Top, Heart, Base notes with visual indicators).
   - Volume size picker (50ml, 100ml, Discovery Set).
   - Accordions for Ingredients, How to Apply, and Olfactory Profile.
   - Verified customer reviews section with submission form.
   - Related fragrances carousel.
5. **Slide-Out Cart Drawer**:
   - Live line-item quantity controls and removal.
   - Free shipping progress bar.
   - Gift wrap / bespoke engraving options.
   - Direct checkout button.
