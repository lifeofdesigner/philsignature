# PHILZ SIGNATURE Luxury Design System

## Aesthetic Philosophy
Inspired by **Dior, Tom Ford, Byredo, and Jo Malone**, PHILZ SIGNATURE embodies haute perfumery:
- **Minimalist Luxury**: High contrast, expansive whitespace, quiet confidence.
- **Editorial Typography**: Pairing stately serif headlines with clean, modern sans-serif body text.
- **Subtle Richness**: Deep obsidian blacks, warm alabaster creams, and muted champagne gold accents.
- **Silky Motion**: Smooth 300-500ms easing transitions with Framer Motion.

---

## Design Tokens

### Color Palette
- **Obsidian Black (`#0D0D0D`)**: Primary dark background and contrast surface.
- **Charcoal (`#161616`)**: Secondary dark surface for cards and dropdowns.
- **Graphite (`#222222`)**: Hover states and subtle dark borders.
- **Border Charcoal (`#2A2A2A`)**: Standard hairline borders.
- **Alabaster Cream (`#FAF8F5`)**: Primary light text, light backgrounds, and cards.
- **Champagne Gold (`#C5A880`)**: Brand signature accent for badges, borders, highlights.
- **Muted Gold (`#8E734F`)**: Secondary gold for subtle secondary states.
- **Sand / Taupe (`#D5CCBF`)**: Muted secondary text.

### Typography
- **Headings**: `Cormorant Garamond`, `Cinzel`, `Playfair Display`, serif.
- **Body & Controls**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`, sans-serif.
- **Code & SKU**: `JetBrains Mono`, monospace.

### Spacing & Elevation
- Minimal border radii (`rounded-none` to `rounded-sm`) to preserve crisp luxury bottle geometry.
- Hairline borders (`border border-luxury-border/60`).
- Subtle amber/gold glow effects (`shadow-[0_0_20px_rgba(197,168,128,0.08)]`).
